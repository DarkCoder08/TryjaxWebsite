What Is the Ready to Rent Program?

Ready to Rent is Tryjax's program for standard apartment unit turns — getting a vacated unit rent-ready for the next resident. A standard turn is completed in 5 business days, once we have access to the unit and your scope is approved.

Every turn is priced flat and confirmed in the scope you approve before work starts, with one point of contact for the job and a photo report on every finished unit.

Frequently Asked Questions

When does the 5-business-day clock start?

When we have access to the unit and the scope is approved.

What does the pilot look like?

Start with 5–10 units. Any standard turn that is not rent-ready in 5 business days is 10% off.

What if a unit needs more than a standard turn?

We will scope it as a Heavy Turn or Value-Add Renovation and send flat pricing for your approval before work starts.
