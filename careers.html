Careers at TryJax

Build great projects with great people.

TryJax delivers commercial construction, renovation, and multi-location development projects across the country. We are interested in dependable professionals who care about safety, communication, craftsmanship, and doing the job right.

View Current Openings Learn About TryJax

Job listings and applications are hosted on an external job platform — you’ll leave tryjax.com when you apply.

Why Work with TryJax?

Nationwide Project Experience

TryJax has completed projects across 41 states for franchise, commercial, renovation, and multi-location clients.

Varied Construction Work

Team members may work across ground-up construction, commercial build-outs, remodeling, installation, and specialty projects.

Quality and Professionalism

TryJax values safe work practices, dependable attendance, clear communication, attention to detail, and quality results.

Opportunities for Experienced Professionals

TryJax considers experienced tradespeople, field leaders, project professionals, and qualified trade partners as company needs develop.

Current Hiring Focus

Our hiring needs change with our project schedule. Visit our current openings page for active positions, locations, requirements, and application instructions.

✔ National traveling carpenters
✔ Tampa Bay and Florida Gulf Coast carpenters
✔ Rough framing and exterior carpentry
✔ Finish carpentry and remodeling
✔ Carpenter foremen and crew leads
✔ Qualified subcontractor carpenter crews

These are common areas of interest, not necessarily individual vacancies. Availability changes by project. A role is considered open only when it appears on our current job-listing page.

Experienced carpenter openings may pay approximately $40–$65 per hour based on demonstrated skill, experience, position, project, and employment arrangement. This range applies to carpenter openings only. Consult the individual job listing for the compensation applicable to that opening.

Local and Traveling Opportunities

Local Opportunities

TryJax may recruit for local projects in Tampa, Bradenton, Parrish, Sarasota, and surrounding Florida Gulf Coast communities. Each listing will identify its location and service-area requirements.

Traveling Opportunities

TryJax performs work throughout the United States and may recruit professionals who can travel to project locations. Travel schedules and arrangements vary and will be explained in the applicable job listing or during the hiring process.

What We Value

✔ Dependable attendance
✔ Safe work practices
✔ Quality craftsmanship
✔ Clear communication
✔ Professional conduct
✔ Attention to detail

Experience matters, but so does how the work gets done. TryJax looks for people who communicate clearly, work safely, respect the project team, and take pride in the finished result.

Work Samples and Qualifications

Depending on the position, qualified candidates may be asked to discuss recent projects, provide professional references, or show examples of completed work. Please submit materials only through the instructions in the official job listing or in response to direct communication from an authorized TryJax representative.

For your safety: do not send Social Security numbers, banking information, identity documents, or other sensitive records through ordinary email.

Interested in Working with TryJax as a Trade Partner?

TryJax also works with qualified subcontractors and established trade crews. Trade-partner opportunities are separate from employee job applications and depend on project needs, location, qualifications, insurance, licensing, availability, and commercial terms.
